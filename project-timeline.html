Delivery plan — waterfall

6-week build, phase by phase

Nine phases, run strictly in sequence — each begins only once the phase before it is signed off, which is the defining trade-off of waterfall: predictable scope and handoffs, at the cost of parallel work. 30 working days total, no slack built in.

Reading this as waterfall, not sprints: each bar starts the day the one above it ends — phase 4 (backend) doesn't begin until phase 3 (frontend) is marked done, even though in practice a team would usually run those in parallel. That sequencing is what makes the 30-day total tight: there's no slack for a phase running long without pushing every phase after it. The four diamonds are the points where the plan assumes a stakeholder sign-off before the next phase starts — the most realistic place for this schedule to slip in practice.
